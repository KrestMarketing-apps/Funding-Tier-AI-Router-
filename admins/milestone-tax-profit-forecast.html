How Milestone pays Funding Tier

From the Independent Marketing Agreement signed 9/10/2026 (Sections 2, 5.6, 9.3; Exhibits D and E) and Milestone's written answers to our due-diligence questions.

  • Phase 1: flat 50% of the collected investigation fee at every tier, paid when the payment clears through UnityFi. Standard fee $500 (confirmed); $995 for urgent or federal + state cases.
  • Phase 2: 40% / 50% / 60% of the resolution fee actually collected. The tier is set by Phase 2 deals funded in the prior calendar month (1–24 / 25–49 / 50+). Tiers reset monthly; crossing a threshold mid-month only changes the following month. New affiliates start at 40%.
  • What "funded" means: the Phase 2 payment, or the first installment, has cleared through UnityFi. A first installment counts toward the tier, but commission is only paid on dollars the client has actually paid.
  • Rate lock: the tier in force when a deal funds applies to every installment on that deal, even if the tier changes later.
  • Installments: paid in the same pattern as the client pays — the locked percentage of each installment as it clears.
  • Pay in full: payout on the discounted price. Loans: standard fee, no discount; funding takes about a week.
  • Nothing is commissionable until it clears: signed agreements, pending or authorized charges and engagements that never fund earn nothing.
  • Suitability call: Milestone's recorded compliance and suitability (welcome) call — after Phase 1 and again after Phase 2 — is a condition before any commission on that client becomes payable.
  • Clawbacks: any payment reversed by chargeback, NSF, refund or cancellation has its commission reversed and offset against future commissions. Refunds caused by our conduct or non-compliance are fully recoverable from us.
  • Refund window: full refund if the client cancels within 15 days of signing, or 3 business days in California, Texas and Indiana. After that the fee is earned. A Phase 1 client whose case doesn't qualify is refunded only inside that window (Milestone says the only disqualifier is too little tax debt).
  • Rolling reserve: Milestone may hold 10% of commissions, released 180 days after they would have been paid, and can hold more against any claim or anticipated loss.
  • Payment schedule: weekly. Payments that clear Saturday–Friday get a statement by the Monday after next and ACH by that Tuesday (Milestone's own example: cleared Oct 13–17 → statement Oct 27 → ACH Oct 29). Dispute a statement within 30 days or it's final.
  • If we exit: either side can end it on 60 days' notice (1-year initial term, then month-to-month). Commissions on active, funded clients keep paying for up to 12 months after termination — forfeited entirely if Milestone terminates for cause. Clients who haven't bought Phase 2 yet go to Milestone.
  • Not shared revenue: tax preparation ($195 per return) and UnityFi processing fees ($4 ACH, 3% card, up to $25 on a return).
  • Eligibility: at least $10,000 owed for federal cases, $20,000 for state-only. No excluded states today.
  • Clients are Milestone's: once accepted, we keep only the commission right and can't contact, solicit or resell the client (2 years in §2.6, 3 years in §10.3). A client who is already a current or former Milestone client earns no commission.

Milestone's reported averages (used as defaults)

  • 70% of funded Phase 1 investigations convert to a funded Phase 2.
  • Phase 2 payment mix: 10–15% pay in full, 35–40% finance with a loan, ~50% installments.
  • About 2.5 weeks from Phase 1 payment clearing to completed investigation. Loans fund in about a week; installment clients should schedule the first payment within 14 days.
  • Case status updates to us within 48 hours / 2 business days. The CRM is portal-only — no webhook or API access.

Modeling assumptions on this page

  • Phase 2 deals fund the month after their Phase 1; month 1 has no prior-month volume, so month 2's deals are at Tier 1.
  • Phase 1 fee is a blend: standard fee × (1 − $995 share) + $995 × $995 share.
  • Balance-band fees are the simple average of the grid rows in each band (pay-in-full the same way).
  • Installment attrition compounds monthly: installment k is collected at (1 − attrition)k. Stopped payments earn nothing.
  • Payout lag moves (lag ÷ 4.35 weeks) of each month's commission into the next month. The reserve comes back 6 months later.
  • Agent pay is a flat share of commission earned — a placeholder until tax rep pay is defined. Lead cost is paid in the month the calls happen.
