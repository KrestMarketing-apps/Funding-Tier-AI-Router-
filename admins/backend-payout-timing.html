⚠  Contract Quotes & Risk Notes
"Marketing Affiliate shall be paid 8.0% upfront of the enrolled debt amount... Payout will be taken back or 'clawed back' if the second payment isn't cleared."Affiliate Agreement §4.1
"June 25th Marketing Affiliate shall be paid 8.0% for all debt that cleared their first monthly draft between June 1st and June 15th. July 10th the Marketing Affiliate shall be paid 8.0% for all debt that cleared their first monthly draft between June 16th and June 30th."Affiliate Agreement §4.1.1 — worked example
Contract inconsistency on file: §4.1's general text says payouts happen "during the first week of the month," which contradicts the §4.1.1 worked example (~25th / ~10th). We are building agent payout timing around the worked example, per internal decision — worth clarifying directly with Level Debt if this matters for a future renegotiation.
Chargeback risk: the full 8.0% upfront payout is clawed back in full if the client's 2nd monthly draft does not clear. This is the fastest-paying backend and the one with the most upfront-payment exposure per file.
