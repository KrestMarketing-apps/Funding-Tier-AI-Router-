How it works

The Mac is the engine. The iPhone just holds the line.

iBluSend runs a bridge app on a Mac that controls native iMessage. Each iPhone only needs an active cellular line, its own Apple ID, and iMessage turned on — which is why an inexpensive used iPhone 6s works fine as a line.

In GoHighLevel

How we use it inside GHL

Once connected, iBluSend behaves like a native channel. Existing workflows start sending blue bubbles, and the unique-contact logic is what makes follow-ups effectively free.

⚙️

Set as telephony provider

GHL sub-account → Settings → Phone System → Additional Settings → Telephony Provider → iBluSend. Conversational AI, Close Bot, and default sends route through iMessage.

🔀

Workflow actions & triggers

Send iMessage / media / voice drops as workflow actions; trigger on iMessage received, read receipt, link clicked, appointment booked.

🔄

Two-way sync

Replies appear in the GHL conversation view in ~5 seconds; contacts stay synced both directions.

🆕

New contact = uses your daily cap

Only contacts you have never messaged before today count against the unique-contact limit (Pro: 150–375/day).

♻️

Engaged contact = free forever

Once messaged on any prior day, a contact is "engaged." Every future message to them is unlimited and does not touch the daily cap.

🕛

Counter resets midnight UTC

That's ~4–5 PM Pacific. A new daily allotment of new-contact slots opens then; engaged follow-ups never wait.

What gets you flagged

Identical message content, fixed send intervals, sending at odd hours, high "STOP"/no-reply rates, and spam reports. Even 2–3 reports in a short window can kill an Apple ID.

✅

What keeps you safe

Warm new lines slowly, vary message content, randomize timing, send in business hours, get explicit consent, and rotate volume across the fleet rather than hammering one line.

Cost per iMessage

Why the per-message cost collapses

Monthly cost is fixed (plan + SIMs; iMessage has no per-message fee). Volume compounds as engaged contacts accumulate and we follow up aggressively — so fixed cost ÷ rising volume drives cost-per-message toward near-zero. Capped at the compliance ceiling so it stays realistic.

Uses the line count, plan mix, and the active compliance posture from the sections above. Deliverability discounts raw capacity into actually-delivered messages once Apple throttling and STOPs are factored in.
