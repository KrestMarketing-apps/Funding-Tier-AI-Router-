Level Debt — Full Commission Policy▾
What is debt settlement?

Level Debt is Funding Tier's debt settlement partner. The client makes monthly payments into a dedicated account while Level Debt negotiates and settles their unsecured debts with creditors over time.

Commission rate

Paid as a percentage of the client's total cleared enrolled debt, at the tier your monthly enrolled volume reaches. Example on a $25,000 deal: Tier 1 = $250, Tier 2 = $287.50, Tier 3 = $325.

When you get paid

After the client completes 2 monthly program payments, commission is paid on the 20th of the following month.

Chargebacks and clawbacks

A client must complete 3 program payments to be fully outside the chargeback window. Cancel after 2 payments → possible clawback. NSF on any payment → possible clawback. 3+ payments or full program completion → no clawback.

Commission modifications

Funding Tier reserves the right to modify commission structures at its sole discretion at any time. Material changes will be communicated.

Debt Validation — Full Commission Policy▾
What is debt validation?

Consumer Shield is Funding Tier's debt validation partner. Debt validation is a legal process where a client's debts are formally challenged and creditors must prove the debt is valid — distinct from debt settlement, which focuses on negotiating a payoff.

When you get paid

Payment 2 payout: the 20th of Month 3. Payment 4 payout (Programs D–I only): the 20th of Month 5.

Chargebacks and clawbacks

Programs D–I: if the client cancels before Payment 4 clears, the Payment 2 commission is subject to clawback within 60 days of cancellation. Programs A, B, C: not subject to clawback — single $150 payment triggers at Payment 2 only.

Bonuses that apply

Debt Validation deals pool together with Debt Resolution deals toward the Daily Hustle and Monthly Volume bonuses, and count toward the Balanced Book Bonus mix. See the Bonuses & SPIFFs tab.

Commission modifications

Funding Tier reserves the right to modify commission rates at its sole discretion at any time. Material changes will be communicated.

Debt Resolution — Full Commission Policy▾
What is debt resolution?

Legacy Capital Services / Elite Legal Practice is Funding Tier's attorney-model debt resolution partner — an alternative path for clients whose debt is too small for Level Debt but who still need professional negotiation.

When you get paid

Payment 2 payout: the 20th of Month 3. Payment 4 payout (Bands L2–L7 only): the 20th of Month 5.

Chargebacks and clawbacks

Bands L2–L7: Payment 2 commission may be clawed back if the client cancels before Payment 4 clears. Band L1: single payout after Payment 2 clears — no clawback.

State eligibility

Legacy Capital Services is currently blocked in Idaho, North Dakota, and Georgia. Route clients in those states to Debt Validation instead.

Bonuses that apply

Debt Resolution deals pool together with Debt Validation deals toward the Daily Hustle and Monthly Volume bonuses, and count toward the Balanced Book Bonus mix. See the Bonuses & SPIFFs tab.

Commission modifications

Funding Tier reserves the right to modify commission rates at its sole discretion at any time. Material changes will be communicated.

Bonus Policy & Accounting Controls▾

All bonus payouts are subject to Funding Tier clawback and chargeback policy.

• Daily, monthly, balanced-book, and Level Debt enrollment bonuses are all subject to review before payout.

• Chargeback liability is enforced daily — any open risk period can delay or reverse a bonus payout.

• Accounting treats bonus amounts as provisional until the related files fully clear the applicable clawback and chargeback windows.

• This policy applies uniformly to avoid reconciliation issues, overpayment errors, and incorrect bonus accruals.

Internal agent reference. Commission rates, bonus thresholds, and payout timing are subject to change at Funding Tier's discretion. Confirm current figures with your team lead before quoting earnings to a client.
