What's needed to build this for real

  • Their compliance script or required verbal disclosures, if the program has an equivalent to the AI compliance call or the ELP verbal disclosures

Once those materials are available, this page gets replaced with the same style of interactive, tab-based SOP as the other two programs — full step-by-step walkthrough, annotated screenshots, compliance callouts, and a support reference section.
